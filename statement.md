# Smart Trip Planner

## Problem Statement

Planning a trip requires users to consider several factors such as destination, travel duration, available budget, accommodation, food, transportation, activities, and personal interests. For beginners or users planning a simple trip, managing these factors manually can be time-consuming and may result in poor budget distribution.

The Smart Trip Planner is designed to provide a simple solution by taking basic travel information from the user and generating a personalized budget plan and day-wise travel plan.

The application divides the user's total budget into different categories and provides travel activities according to the selected travel preference. It also calculates the estimated daily budget and provides basic travel advice.


## Scope of the Project

The scope of the Smart Trip Planner includes:

1. Collecting basic travel information from the user.
2. Accepting the destination and duration of the trip.
3. Accepting the total available travel budget.
4. Providing different travel styles such as:
    (a) Budget
    (b) Balanced
    (c) Luxury
5. Providing different travel preferences such as:
    (a) Adventure
    (b) Nature
    (c) History
    (d) Food
    (e) Mixed
6. Automatically distributing the budget among major travel expenses.
7. Calculating the estimated budget available per day.
8. Generating a simple day-wise travel activity plan.
9. Providing basic travel and budget management advice.


## Target Users

The Smart Trip Planner is intended for:

### Students

Students can use the application to create simple travel plans while working within a limited budget.

### Budget Travelers

Users who want to divide their available money among transportation, accommodation, food, activities, and emergency expenses.

### Beginner Travelers

People who need a simple starting point for organizing their trip.

### Families and Groups

Users can use the application to create a basic travel budget and activity plan before making detailed arrangements.

## Project Objective

The main objective of the Smart Trip Planner is to demonstrate how basic Python programming can be used to solve a practical problem by combining user input, decision-making, calculations, and loops to generate a simple personalized travel plan.
